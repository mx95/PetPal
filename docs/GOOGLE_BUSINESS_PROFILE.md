# Google Business Profile — PetPal Care Hub

Use this guide to list **PetPal Care Hub** on Google Maps and Google Search (business panel beside results).

Legal operator: **Pomware Innovative Solutions Ltd** · Website: **https://petpal.com.cy** · Email: **info@petpal.com.cy**

---

## Before you start

Have ready:

- Business name to display: **PetPal Care Hub**
- Legal name (if asked): **Pomware Innovative Solutions Ltd**
- Address in Cyprus (or “service area” if online-only)
- Phone number (mobile is fine)
- Website: `https://petpal.com.cy`
- Logo / cover photo (square logo + optional banner)
- Email access for verification (postcard, phone, or video)

---

## Step 1 — Open Google Business Profile

1. On your phone or computer, go to **[business.google.com](https://business.google.com)**
2. Sign in with the **Google account** you use for Search Console (same account is easiest)
3. Tap **Manage now** or **Add business**

---

## Step 2 — Create the listing

### Business name
Enter exactly:

**PetPal Care Hub**

(Do not add keywords like “best GPS tracker” — Google may suspend the listing.)

### Business category
Pick the closest match, for example:

- **Pet store** — if you sell NFC tags / trackers online, or  
- **Software company** — if Google offers it and you’re mainly an app/platform, or  
- **Pet supply store**

You can add a second category later (e.g. “Pet sitter” is wrong; avoid unrelated categories).

### Do you have a location customers can visit?

| Your situation | Choose |
|----------------|--------|
| Physical shop/office clients visit | **Yes** → enter your Nicosia address |
| Online-only (ship tags, no walk-in) | **No** → then set **Service area** (e.g. Cyprus or Nicosia + cities you deliver to) |

Many online shops select **No** for storefront and define **Cyprus** as the service area.

### Contact details
- **Website:** `https://petpal.com.cy`
- **Phone:** your business mobile
- **Email:** `info@petpal.com.cy` (if prompted)

---

## Step 3 — Verify the business

Google will offer one or more methods:

| Method | What happens |
|--------|----------------|
| **Postcard** | Mailed to your address with a code (5–14 days) |
| **Phone / SMS** | Instant code to your mobile |
| **Email** | Link or code to your domain email |
| **Video verification** | Short video showing signage, products, or website (common for online businesses) |

For an **online-only** Cyprus business, **email** or **video** verification is common.

After verification, your profile goes **live** on Google Maps and can appear in brand searches.

---

## Step 4 — Complete the profile (important for search)

In the Business Profile dashboard, fill in:

1. **Description** (750 chars max), e.g.  
   *PetPal Care Hub — GPS pet trackers, NFC ID tags, nearby vets and pet services, and bookings. Serving pet owners across Cyprus. Shop at petpal.com.cy*

2. **Hours** — if no physical hours, set “Open 24 hours” or by appointment

3. **Photos** — logo, product photos (NFC tags, tracker), app screenshots

4. **Products / Services** (if available) — e.g. “NFC Pet Tag”, “GPS Tracker Subscription”

5. **Attributes** — online appointments, delivery, etc. where applicable

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Don’t** create duplicate listings (one for PetPal, one for Care Hub)
- **Don’t** use a fake address — use service area if you’re online-only
- **Don’t** keyword-stuff the business name
- **Do** keep name, website, and email consistent everywhere
